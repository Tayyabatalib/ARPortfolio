Bridging Climate Science with Water Security

Dr. Akif Rahim is a seasoned Hydro-Climate Data Scientist specializing in climate extremes, hydrological modeling, ecosystem impacts, and climate-change assessment. He is proficient in Python and R for hydrological simulation, runoff generation and routing, drought and flood assessment, and regional climate-impact modeling.

He has hands-on experience with top-tier hydrological models — VIC, HEC-HMS, and CLIMADA — and advanced AI/ML applications in hydrology and climate science. He developed PakDMS, an operational drought monitoring and decision-support system, contributed to the Pakistan Water Body Inventory, and collaborated with Google Asia-Pacific on an AI-driven reservoir-operation and flood-forecasting system now operational with the Punjab Irrigation Department.

Core Specialization

Where Data Science Meets Water & Climate

Six domains where analysis turns into early warnings, resilient infrastructure, and smarter water management.

AI & Machine Learning in Hydrology

Artificial intelligence and ML for hydrological modeling — from ANN sediment-runoff models to AI surrogate calibration of the VIC model and AI-driven reservoir operations built with Google Asia-Pacific.

Flood Forecasting & Risk Analysis

River-flow forecasting across Indus Basin rim stations, SAR-based flood-depth estimation, flood-inundation mapping, and routing models — the work behind life-saving forecasts in 2023 and 2025.

Flash Droughts & Ecosystem Response

PhD research unraveling rapid-onset droughts across Central Europe — ecosystem resistance, resilience, and the space–time dynamics of flash drought propagation.

Climate Downscaling & Adaptation

Non-hydrostatic dynamic downscaling (NHRCM) producing 5 km × 5 km climate projections for the Indus Basin with Japan's Meteorological Research Institute — assessing future water availability.

WEFE Nexus

Water–Energy–Food–Ecosystem integration under the CGIAR Nexus Gains Initiative — linking downscaled climate projections with hydro-economic models for Indus Basin planning.

Decision Support Systems

Designing operational tools that put science in decision-makers' hands — PakDMS for national drought monitoring and the Pakistan Water Bodies Inventory, both live today.
