Professional Profile

From Indus Flood Rooms to European Flash-Drought Research

As a seasoned Hydro-Climate Data Scientist, Dr. Akif Rahim specializes in climate extremes, hydrological modeling, ecosystem impacts, and climate-change assessment. He is proficient in Python and R for hydrological simulation, runoff generation and routing, drought and flood assessment, and regional climate-impact modeling using mathematical, numerical, and computational methods.

He has hands-on experience with top-tier hydrological models — VIC, HEC-HMS, and CLIMADA — and advanced AI/ML applications in hydrology and climate science. He developed PakDMS, an operational drought-monitoring and decision-support system, contributed to the Pakistan Water Body Inventory, and collaborated with Google Asia-Pacific on an AI-driven reservoir-operation and flood-forecasting system, currently operational with the Punjab Irrigation Department.

He served as a visiting researcher at the Japan Meteorological Research Institute (MRI), contributing to 5 km × 5 km high-resolution climate projections for the Indus Basin, and taught Exploratory Data Analysis and Environmental Data Presentation to MSc students at the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague.

His flood forecasts during the 2023 and 2025 flood events earned a formal Letter of Appreciation from the Government of Punjab for safeguarding lives and critical infrastructure.
