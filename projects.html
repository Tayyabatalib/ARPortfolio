PakDMS — Pakistan Drought Monitoring & Decision Support System

Pakistan's national drought monitoring platform. Designed and developed the drought-forecasting module for the Pakistan Meteorological Department using the Variable Infiltration Capacity (VIC) hydrological model with AI-based surrogate modeling for efficient calibration — operational under the FCDO (UK) funded project.
